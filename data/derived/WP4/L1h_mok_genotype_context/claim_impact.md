## Verdict (2026-09-04): OPPOSITE SIGN, and it makes biological sense
- 5/7 contrasts NEGATIVE (Asn-rich proteins relatively DEPLETED in K13 mutants at steady state),
  strongest: Troph_E1_C580Y rho=-0.129 p=3.5e-10; Rings_E1_R539T rho=-0.116 p=3.6e-9.
- Interpretation: acute starvation = MOBILIZE Asn-rich regulators (L1g +0.167); chronic ART-resistant
  steady state = ECONOMIZE the expensive Asn-rich proteome (Mok 2021's own headline: rewired, slower-growth
  metabolism with reduced protein turnover). Same axis, opposite regime.
- No contradiction with L1g (acute vs chronic are different questions); strengthens the claim that the
  Asn-composition axis is the layer through which ART resistance is implemented.
