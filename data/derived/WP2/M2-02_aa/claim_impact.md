# M2-02 Claim Impact — Amino-Acid Counterfactual (Gate B)

## Summary verdict
**Genomic GC explains ~78.7% (median) of amino-acid CLR variation.** This is a **compositional null confirmation at the AA layer**, not a discovery of genome-wide AA adaptation. The GC coupling itself is significant for 14/20 AAs (OLS slope `q<0.05` masked, BH FDR) and **13/14 are robust** across LOO — i.e. these 13 are the **GC-coupled set** (the composition axis itself), not residuals beyond background. Per-species residuals (`resid_masked`, Cohen's d) were computed but not used for candidate nomination in this release. The AA layer **cannot license a genome-wide adaptive amino-acid claim at Gate B without M3 supply/demand integration** — it narrows to a translation-primary vs compositional-maintenance boundary (see Gate).

## Evidence

- **Background** `M2-02_background_explained.tsv` 20 AAs (OLS `CLR ~ genome_gc`, n=16, BH FDR per track):
  median `R2_masked = 0.7874` (unmasked 0.7866), mean `R2_masked = 0.595`. Median > mean indicates a heavy tail: a subset of AAs are almost fully GC-determined (G R2=0.954, A 0.921, N 0.910) while 6/20 are GC-indifferent (C/E/H/L/M/T R2<0.27). `cds_gc` parallel is near-identical (median R2 cds_gc masked 0.843, consistent with GC compositional coupling). Removing any clade: LOO median R2 0.746–0.837 (full 0.787), **gradient retained in all 7 clades**; coccidian removal most sensitive (delta -0.041) and rodent+piroplasm most stable.
- **GC-coupling significance** (OLS slope p per AA for `CLR ~ genome_gc`, BH `q<0.05` masked primary) — note this tests the **background coupling itself**, not deviation from it:
  `14/20 masked q<0.05`, `14/20 unmasked q<0.05` — identical sets. Not coupled: C (q0.382), E (0.146), H (0.564), L (0.778), M (0.0546 borderline), T (0.0567 borderline). M/T are one threshold-step from significance and must not be over-interpreted as "adaptive" or "neutral" without M3.
- **Robustness** `M2-02_robustness_by_aa.tsv` 04 §9 definition (full FDR<0.05 & masked/unmasked same sign & ≥5/7 LOO `p<0.05` + same direction, OLS refit per subset):
  **ROBUST = 13/20**: A, R, N, D, Q, G, I, K, F, P, W, Y, V — 93% of GC-coupled AAs.
  **SENSITIVE = 1/20**: S (`q=0.045` but only 4/7 LOO significant + same direction).
  **NOT_SIGNIFICANT (not GC-coupled) = 6/20**: C, E, H, L, M, T. No AA flips direction between masked/unmasked (all 20 direction-consistent).
- **Phylogeny-controlled check** `M2-02_phylogeny_gls.tsv` (Brownian V from `M1-02_species_tree.nwk`, GLS `beta=(X'V^{-1}X)^{-1}X'V^{-1}y`, n=16):
  GLS is secondary diagnostic; OLS r and GLS beta are sign-consistent for all 13 robust AAs. **Disclosure (post-Gate-B audit):** the M1-02 k-mer tree is distance-saturated so V ≈ diagonal — `p_gls` equals OLS `p_masked` to ~6 digits, i.e. the phylogenetic correction is near-vacuous at this divergence scale and should not be cited as independent evidence. The GC–AA coupling is consistent with a genome-wide compositional process rather than a single-clade innovation (also supported by LOO, which does refit).
- **LCR stratification** `M2-02_structural_stratification.tsv` + qc:
  global LCR `0.184%` (105,909 / 57,466,941 residues, window64 entropy<1.5 + numba jit). Masked vs unmasked median R2 delta `0.00078`, significance sets identical. **LCR does not drive the headline number**; dual-track retained and **Gate B uses masked** per contract. Structural heuristics (KD window19>1.6, signal N30 window8>1.8, length quartiles, LCR bins) cover `3521` stratum×AA rows — **descriptive counts only, no cross-stratum statistical control was performed** (audit correction: earlier "survives structural stratification" wording overstated this); formal InterPro domains `DEFERRED_TO_M3` by design (04 §7 prohibits L3 upgrade).
- **Site-level** `M2-02_site_level_residual.tsv`:
  100 single-copy core OGs sampled, centre-star SP001 pairwise alignment (`PairwiseAligner`, BLOSUM62 gap -10/-0.5) as MAFFT fallback. **QC passes**: `gap_frac_mean 0.0119`, `mean_identity 0.840` (thresholds gap>0.4 or ident<0.25 would defer). Status `PLACEHOLDER_QC_PASS` — the 5 rows carry OG IDs only with **empty numeric fields** (audit fix 2026-09-03: previous version emitted synthetic gap 0.1 / entropy 1.2 placeholder values, now removed). Full per-site MSA and parsimony ancestor (`most frequent AA per site`) deferred pending MAFFT. Site layer is **not licensed for mechanism claims** in this release.
- **Orthogroups**
  `fallback_python_kmerRBH_v1` (2-mer 400-dim cosine top5 + length tolerance 0.30 + SP001-anchored RBH single-linkage, `USE_ALIGN=False`): **3647 OGs ≥4 species, 181 single-copy core (16×1), mean OG size 9.52**. Reusable cache (`REUSE_ORTHO`). Kmer-only prefilter inflates false-positive OG risk vs diamond+MCL — mitigated by reporting only per-AA compositional regression (OG-agnostic CLR) as primary inference and using core OGs only for site QC.

## Gate
- **Gate B on AA counterfactual alone: GO with compositional null, NO-GO for genome-wide adaptive AA claim.** The 13 robust AAs enter M3 as the **GC-coupled layer** on which demand-driven departures would have to appear (04 §9 steps 1–2: coupling stable, not single-clade/LCR driven) but must not be presented as C3-level translation adaptation without independent supply/demand evidence in M3.
- **Conditional GO boundary for translation-primary claim** (03 Roadmap): requires in M3 (i) `D_{c,s,t}` demand correlates with residual beyond GC, (ii) tRNA supply / charging / modification predicts same-direction AA residual, and (iii) cross-lineage stability (LOO analogue at gene/tissue level). Absent that, the AA layer is **DOWNGRADE to compositional maintenance** for manuscript C2.
- Negative-result boundary stated: 6/20 null + 1 sensitive + 2 borderline is real and informative — genome-wide AA adaptation is not detected here; locus-specific effects remain possible with functional integration.

## Limitations & review risk
- **Orthogroup fallback** `kmerRBH_v1` without diamond/mcl/mafft — kmer cosine alone decides best hit (`USE_ALIGN=False`). False-positive OG rate higher than OrthoFinder; primary inference is deliberately OG-agnostic (per-species CLR) to bound blast radius; site layer explicitly minimal. Reviewer challenge anticipated — mitigated by frozen `orthogroups.tsv` (34713 rows), `single_copy_core.list` (181), method tag `fallback_python_kmerRBH_v1`, and `REUSE_ORTHO` reproducibility.
- **Site-level minimal reporting** — 100 OGs sampled but `M2-02_site_level_residual.tsv` carries only 5 placeholder rows with **empty numerics** (audit fix 2026-09-03: earlier version emitted synthetic `gap 0.1 entropy 1.2` values; removed). Full per-site residuals + BLOSUM-aware ancestor deferred pending MAFFT. Contract QC passes (gap 1.2% ident 84%, computed from real pairwise alignments) so status is `PLACEHOLDER_QC_PASS`, not `DEFERRED` — site layer not licensed for mechanism claims.
- **Structural heuristics only** — KD19>1.6 / signal N30 window8>1.8 are lightweight, not InterPro/AlphaFold/pLM; formal domains `DEFERRED_TO_M3` per 04 §5. Stratification table is descriptive counts only; no cross-stratum statistical control was performed.
- **PGLS near-vacuous** — distance-saturated k-mer tree makes V ≈ diagonal; GLS p == OLS p to ~6 digits. Phylogenetic independence of the GC coupling rests on LOO refits (real) rather than GLS (decorative at this scale).
- **Significance = coupling, not residual** — all M2-02 p/q values test the `CLR ~ genome_gc` slope (background itself). Per-species residual outliers (Cohen's d) computed in `M2-02_aa_residual_table.tsv` but intentionally unused for candidate nomination until M3 demand data exist.
- **16 species with protein only** — SP009/SP010 `has_cds=false` in tree only (frozen invariant); n=16 limits PGLS power (reported alongside OLS, not primary).
- **No D006–D025 / tRNA / proteome / population / MCA** downloaded or used — by Gate B contract; second-layer evidence is M3 scope.

## Downstream
M3 supply/demand (`D_{c,s,t}`) must test whether the 13 robust AA residuals predict expression-weighted AA demand beyond `genome_gc`, with tRNA abundance/charging/modification and ribosome/mRNA stability as second evidence layer (04 §9 step 3+). S (sensitive) and M/T (borderline) re-tested only if M3 functional hit emerges.
